GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might wonder.

Does requesting a booking confirm my appointment?

Not immediately. A request is sent first. The garage needs to accept it before it becomes confirmed.

Can I use Heeben without sharing my GPS location?

Yes. You can type your locality or area manually. Location access is optional.

Are the garages shown here verified?

Garages sign up and list themselves. Listings marked Sample are demo businesses. Ratings only come from customers whose booking the garage marked as completed.

How do I pay?

You pay the garage directly after the service. Heeben does not take payments yet.

I run a garage. How do I get listed?

Sign up with a garage-owner account, add your services and prices, and customers can start sending you booking requests.

THE FINE PRINT

Terms & privacy.

What Heeben does

Heeben connects vehicle owners with independent garages. A booking is a request: it is only confirmed once the garage accepts it. Garages set their own prices and are responsible for their work. Prices shown are indicative starting prices.

Payments

Heeben does not process payments. You pay the garage directly.

Your data

We store your name, email, phone number, vehicles and bookings so the service can work. A booking shares your name, phone, vehicle and notes with that garage only. Passwords are stored hashed. Your location is only used in your browser to sort garages by distance and is never stored by us.

Reviews

Only customers with a completed booking can leave a review. Do not post unlawful or abusive content.

This is a starter policy. Have it reviewed by a lawyer before public launch.
