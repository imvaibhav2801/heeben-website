IDEAS INTO IMPACT

A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curiosity, technology
and the drive to build.

I’m Vaibhav, a first-year engineering student with a strong interest in technology, entrepreneurship and building solutions to real-world problems. I believe meaningful businesses begin by understanding everyday challenges and finding better ways to solve them.

Heeben started with a simple thought: taking care of a vehicle should not feel complicated. Finding the right service, understanding the options and connecting with a dependable service provider should be easier for every vehicle owner.

THE VISION FOR HEEBEN

Care that goes beyond the drive.

Heeben is being built with the ambition to create a simpler, smarter and more accessible vehicle-care experience. The long-term vision is a connected ecosystem that brings vehicle owners, trusted service providers and useful automotive solutions closer together.

We’re starting with the essentials, learning from customers and service partners, and improving one step at a time. The goal is to build something useful, dependable and worthy of people’s trust.

“We Care Beyond the Drive.”
